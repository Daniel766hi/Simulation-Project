Mine Production & Cost Simulator

A discrete-time simulation of an open-pit style mine: reserve depletion, ramp-up, equipment availability, grade and price variability, and price-shock events, run month by month over the chosen horizon. Adjust the variables on the left and re-run.

Simulation output over time

Each dot = one simulated mine life (random grade, availability, price path & shocks)

X-axis: total metal produced over the horizon. Y-axis: final cumulative profit. Each trial re-draws grade, availability and price-path noise from the same distributions used in the time-series tab, so the spread of dots shows how sensitive the project's profitability is to operational and market uncertainty — the same idea as scenario/sensitivity analysis, just visualized as a scatter instead of a table.
Client-side simulation — nothing is sent to a server. Model assumptions documented in README.md.
