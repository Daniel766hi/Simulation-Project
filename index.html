Monte Carlo risk analysis

Each dot is one simulated mine life. Green = value-accretive (NPV > 0), red = value-destroying.

X-axis: total metal produced over the horizon. Y-axis: project NPV after initial capex, royalties and tax. Each trial re-draws grade, availability and price-path noise from the same distributions used in the time-series tab, so the spread of dots shows how sensitive the investment case is to operational and market uncertainty. The headline number is the probability of a positive NPV — a project with a healthy expected NPV but only a 55% chance of clearing zero is a very different proposition from one at 95%.
