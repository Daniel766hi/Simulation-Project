Mine Production & Cost Simulator

A discrete-time simulation of an open-pit style mine: reserve depletion, ramp-up, equipment availability, grade and price variability, and price-shock events, run month by month and carried through a full cash-flow waterfall — revenue, operating cost, royalties, tax, sustaining capex — to NPV, IRR and payback on invested capital. Adjust the variables on the left and re-run.

Scenario comparison

Each dot = one simulated mine life. Green = value-accretive (NPV > 0), red = value-destroying.

X-axis: total metal produced over the horizon. Y-axis: project NPV after initial capex, royalties and tax. Each trial re-draws grade, availability and price-path noise from the same distributions used in the time-series tab, so the spread of dots shows how sensitive the investment case is to operational and market uncertainty — the same idea as scenario/sensitivity analysis, just visualized as a scatter instead of a table. The headline number is the probability of a positive NPV: a project with a healthy expected NPV but only a 55% chance of clearing zero is a very different proposition from one at 95%.
Client-side simulation — nothing is sent to a server. Model assumptions documented in README.md.
