Supply Chain Agent Model

Where the bullwhip effect comes from

Mine economics Supply chain ABM Bass diffusion M5 forecasting

Four independent agents — retailer, wholesaler, distributor and factory — each order stock using only what they can see: their own inventory and the orders from the customer directly downstream. Nobody is told to overreact. The bullwhip effect emerges anyway, and you can watch it happen, then switch on the mitigations and measure how much of it goes away.
