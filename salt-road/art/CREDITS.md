# The Salt Road — story-card art credits

Full-screen illustrations shown behind story cards and the title screen. Every source was checked on its own OpenGameArt page; only CC0 and CC-BY 3.0/4.0 assets are used.

All images were scaled and cropped to 1600x900 and given the same colour grade (saturation -15%, midtones slightly darkened, cool-purple shadow tint toward #1b1633), then saved as WebP quality 90. Per-file details are in the Edits column and in `manifest.json`.

## Fit notes

Where an image only partly matches its brief:

- **prologue**: Daytime desert market street (painted battle-back); reads as a desert village rather than dusk.
- **ch9_coast**: Dark sea cave on a rocky coast at night; there is no shipwreck in the image.
- **act2_rains**: Rain clouds with falling rain streaks only; no ground visible.
- **rime**: Frozen, snow-banked river (photo processed into a painted style by the author); not a lake.
- **weir**: Canyon with a river/lake and city lights; no dam.
- **epilogue**: Grassy hill at sunrise with a lone figure and standing stone; a hill path rather than a road.
- **home**: Brighter, more cartoon-like than the rest of the set.
- **ending**: Sun low over marsh water; painted as a sunset but works as sunrise.
- **ch12_king**: Grey abyssal cavern floor with light shafts; no underwater ruins.

## Skipped

- **title**: an illustration was found ("Foggy Scene" by zonked, CC0) but not used: the title screen keeps the game's own pixel-art scene.

- **ch6_gate** (a great city gate in rain): no CC0/CC-BY painted or pixel-art scene of a city gate was found. The closest results were GPL-only ("Castle Gate" PSD), CC-BY-SA ("Fortress background" gate image), or tilesets/sprites rather than backgrounds.
- **cistern** (an underground cistern or flooded hall with columns): no suitable licence-clean image was found. The best candidate, a flat stone-floor battle backdrop with no columns or water, was rejected as a poor fit. "Sewer Level Background" (CC-BY-SA) and "Tileable Interior Mine Background" (CC-BY-SA) were excluded on licence.

No source image is used for more than one key. Three packs supply two keys each from different images: "3 Halloween Game Backgrounds" (ch2_well, ch8_marsh), "Oil painting landscapes" (drysea, ending) and "Concept Art Studies Bundle 1" (ch3_grove, ch9_coast).
