# The Salt Road — Audio Credits

All files in this folder were downloaded from the asset's own page, whose license was checked on that page (November 2026), and converted to Ogg Vorbis with ffmpeg. Only CC0 and CC-BY assets are used; nothing here is NC, ND, SA or GPL.

## Skipped

- **kenney.nl** (RPG Audio, Impact Sounds, Interface Sounds pages): the site returned HTTP 403 to automated downloads, so nothing was taken from kenney.nl directly. RPG Audio and Interface Sounds were instead taken from Kenney's own uploads on OpenGameArt (same author, CC0, verified on those pages). **Impact Sounds** is not on Kenney's OpenGameArt account, so hit/impact sounds come from other CC0 packs (StarNinjas, rubberduck).
- **Spell Sounds Starter Pack**, **3 Heal Spells**, **Heal** and **Jingling heal** (OpenGameArt): rejected — CC-BY-SA / GPL only.
- **FreePD**: not needed; every music key was filled by CC-BY 4.0 tracks from incompetech.
