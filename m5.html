M5 Demand Forecasting

Walmart unit sales, 42,840 series, 28 days ahead

Mine economics Supply chain ABM Bass diffusion M5 forecasting

A full solution to both tracks of the Kaggle M5 Forecasting competition, Accuracy and Uncertainty (US$100,000 prize pool across the two): forecast daily unit sales of 3,049 Walmart products in 10 stores across California, Texas and Wisconsin for the next 28 days. It is scored on the competition's own metric and its private-leaderboard days, which were used once, at the end. The results are then turned into a business question: how much stock does a better forecast save?
